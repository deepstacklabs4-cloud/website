Privacy Policy

Table Reads: Live Poker Notes · Effective November 18, 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Table Reads: Live Poker Notes (“Table Reads” or “the app”), made by Deep Stack Labs (“we” or “us”), handles your information. The short version: what you enter stays on your phone. We don't run accounts, ads, or analytics, and we never sell your data.

At a glance

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • Your player notes, hands, sessions, and ranges are stored only on your phone.
  • The only time the app sends anything over the internet is when you choose to send us feedback.
  • We do not sell or share your information for advertising.

1. Information you enter in the app

Table Reads lets you save things like player names or nicknames, tags, notes and descriptions, hands you record, poker session results (such as stakes, locations, buy-ins, and cash-outs), and range charts you build. All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Because notes may be about other people at your table, please be thoughtful about what you write.

2. Backups you choose to make

You can save a backup file to your phone or send it through Android's share menu, for example to your own Google Drive or to email. These backups only happen when you tap the button, and they go only where you choose. The backup file is then handled by that service under its own privacy policy. We never receive a copy.

If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may also include the app's data in your personal Google backup. That is controlled by your phone settings and your Google account, not by us.

3. Feedback you send us

If you open Send feedback and tap Send, the app sends:

  • the message you typed,
  • your email address, only if you choose to enter one so we can reply,
  • the app's version number, and
  • your phone's make and model (for example, “Samsung SM-S911U”), to help us fix bugs.

This is delivered to our support email through Web3Forms, a form-to-email service. We use it only to read and answer your feedback and improve the app. We keep feedback emails only as long as they are useful for support, and you can ask us to delete them at any time. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. You can also email us directly instead.

4. App lock

If you turn on the app lock, the app asks Android to check your fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, or password. Android handles this check itself and only tells the app whether it succeeded. The app never sees or stores your biometric data or your screen lock.

5. Permissions

The app uses the internet permission only to send feedback when you tap Send. It does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or other personal data on your phone.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only third party involved is Web3Forms, which delivers feedback you choose to send. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete players, notes, hands, and sessions inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of its data from your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them. To have feedback you sent us deleted, email support@deepstacklabs.com.

8. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Feedback is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. You can add the app lock for extra protection on a shared phone.

9. Children

Table Reads is a tool for adult poker players and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us feedback, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Since the only information we receive is feedback you send, email us and we will help.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. Big changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
