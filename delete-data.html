Delete Your Data

Table Reads: Live Poker Notes · by Deep Stack Labs

This page explains how to delete your data from Table Reads: Live Poker Notes, an Android app made by Deep Stack Labs.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@deepstacklabs.com with the subject Data deletion request.
  2. Send it from the email address you entered in the app's feedback form. If you did not enter an email address, tell us roughly when you sent feedback and what it said, so we can find it.
  3. Ask us to delete your data. We will confirm by email once it is done.

What we delete

The only data Deep Stack Labs receives is feedback you choose to send from Send feedback in the app. It is delivered to us through Web3Forms, a form-to-email service. When you ask, we delete:

  • your feedback messages,
  • the email address you entered in the feedback form, if any, and
  • the app version and phone make and model sent with that feedback.

How long it takes, and what is kept

We delete your feedback data within 30 days of your request. Nothing is kept afterward.

Data stored on your phone

Everything else you enter in Table Reads, such as players, tags, notes, hands, sessions, and range charts, is stored only on your phone. We never receive it, so you delete it yourself:

  • delete individual players, notes, hands, or sessions inside the app,
  • clear the app's data in Android Settings (Apps, then Table Reads, then Storage, then Clear data), or
  • uninstall the app.

Any backup files you saved or shared yourself stay where you put them until you delete them.
