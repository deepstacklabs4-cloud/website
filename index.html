Table Reads: Live Poker Notes

An Android app for live poker players.

Table Reads helps you remember the players at your table. Tag a player, jot a quick note, and their reads are there the next time you sit down with them. You can keep it that simple, or turn on more: hand recording, session results, and your own range charts.

Your reads stay on your phone

There is no account to make, no ads, and no tracking. Everything you enter is stored on your own device. Read our privacy policy.
