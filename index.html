An Android app for live poker players

Table Reads: Live Poker Notes

Remember the players at your table. Tag a player, jot a quick note, and your reads are waiting the next time you sit down with them.

Currently in closed testing

As simple or as detailed as you like

Start with quick tags and notes. Turn on more features only if you want them, and switch them off again any time.

Player tags and notes

Label players as tight, loose, aggressive, and more, and add notes on how they play. Don't know a name? Save them by seat and name them later.

Hand recorder

Record a hand street by street, with bet sizes in big blinds, board cards, and showdowns, then review a clear summary afterward.

Session results

Track buy-ins, rebuys, cash-outs, stakes, and locations, and see how you are doing per hour.

Your own range charts

Build, color, and save your own preflop range charts by position and situation, and pull them up at the table.

Your reads stay on your phone

No account to make, no ads, and no tracking. Everything you enter is stored on your own device, and backups only go where you choose to send them.
